description=Assisted CAD/DXF processing and preliminary drainage sector generation for mining and geotechnical engineering.
about=RMA GeoHydro imports CAD/DXF geometry, classifies drainage features and creates preliminary drainage sectors between channel centerlines and transverse dividers or downchutes. Canal and transverse CAD layers are selected interactively. The plugin also exports vector files and reports geometric gaps. Experimental software: preliminary sectors are not hydrologically validated catchments and must be checked before engineering use.
